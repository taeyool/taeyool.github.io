About

I am a Ph.D. student in the School of Computing at KAIST, advised by Prof. Hongseok Yang. I am interested in making formal mathematics more scalable and practical, from automating proof development to making large machine-checked proofs cheaper to build and check. My recent work formalizes flag algebras in Lean 4 and uses it to verify new bounds in extremal graph theory.

  • Formal verification
  • Computer-assisted proofs
  • Lean 4
  • Flag algebras
  • Extremal combinatorics
